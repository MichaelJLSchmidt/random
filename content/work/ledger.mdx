## The problem

Small non-profits run on volunteers who change every year. Their bookkeeping lived in spreadsheets that only one person understood, and closing the month meant waiting for that person.

## What we changed

Ledger turns the month into a short checklist. Each step explains what it is for, shows what is still missing and can be handed to someone else halfway through.

- Plain-language categories that map to the accountant's codes behind the scenes
- A review screen that lists only what changed since last month
- Roles that let a treasurer approve without being able to edit

## How it was built

We built a small design system alongside the app: tokens, a handful of components and written guidance on tone. Every component was tested with a screen reader and with keyboard only before it was used in a screen.

## Outcome

Pilot organisations closed their month in an evening instead of a week, and three of them handed the role to a new volunteer without outside help.
