## The problem

Survey teams on the coast spend whole days out of signal. The old process was paper forms, photographed at night and typed up the next week. Observations were lost, duplicated or attached to the wrong site.

## What we changed

We designed the app around the moment of recording, not the moment of syncing. Every screen works offline. A record is saved the instant a field is filled, and the app shows plainly which records are on the phone and which are already on the server.

- One-handed entry, with large targets that work with wet gloves
- A site picker that uses the last known position and never blocks on GPS
- A sync log written in plain sentences instead of status codes

## How it was built

The front end is a progressive web app with a local database and a queue for uploads. Conflicts are rare but real, so the app keeps both versions and asks a person to choose, with the differences side by side.

## Outcome

In the first season, transcription work dropped from about a week per trip to none. Teams reported fewer missing records and, more importantly, trusted that what they entered would arrive.
